About Me

Yaashvi Kedia is a conductor, pianist and educator based in Mumbai. She is a passionate artist who started at the piano at the age of 11, explored through singing, and then found herself in conducting while studying at KM Music Conservatory.

Conductor

In 2026 Yaashvi conducted the finale of Tchaikovsky’s Symphony No. 4 with the Juan José Landaeta Symphony Orchestra in Venezuela, in a masterclass with Jesús Uzcategui, and worked with the Filarmonica Pitești in Romania under Michalis Economou.

Before that, she conducted a piano concerti programme with KM Music Conservatory at the Goethe-Institut in Chennai (2025), and was Co-Director and Conductor of the Middlesex Chamber Orchestra in London (2023 to 2024). In 2023 she led an 80-member orchestra and choir at the Concert for Friendship, with KM Music Conservatory, the British Council and Big Noise Scotland. In 2022 she conducted A.R. Rahman’s Sunshine Orchestra with guitarist Matt Bacon.

Her roots are in choral music. She conducted the KM Chamber Choir, a cultural choir for the embassies of Mexico, Chile, Colombia and Peru, and the Chennai Chorale Society in chorales from Bach’s St John Passion.

Yaashvi studied at KM Music Conservatory and at Middlesex University, London. Charlotte Corderoy has been her conducting mentor since 2023, and she has also learned from Dr. James Bunch, Alice Farnham and Daryl Griffith.

Educator

Yaashvi has been teaching piano since 2020, to students aged 7 to 75. She taught in the Preparatory Department at KM Music Conservatory and has trained teachers for Furtados School of Music.

At City Montessori School, Lucknow, she built the orchestral and choral programmes, and she now returns as a consultant for teacher training and conducting.

She co-founded Maestera, a music education platform that connects students and educators, with her brother. She also runs Opera Sundays, online opera watch-parties, and is developing the School of Orchestra, an initiative to build lasting orchestral ecosystems.

Pianist and more

As a pianist, Yaashvi has performed at events for the Mexican and Brazilian embassies, and she plays chamber music, from Mozart to Piazzolla to Louise Farrenc.

She also arranges and composes. In 2024 she orchestrated music by composer Kavi Pau for a performance at the Southbank Centre, London. Her own pieces bring Indian raags into the English choral tradition, starting with Fantasy in Raag Yaman.

Her projects include The Lost Woman, which brings back classical music by women composers, and Anubhuti, a collaboration with movement artist Claire Loussouarn.
