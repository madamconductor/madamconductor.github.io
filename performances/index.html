Symphony No. 4, finale, Tchaikovsky Watch
Juan José Landaeta Symphony Orchestra
Venezuela
An El Sistema orchestral masterclass with Jesús Uzcategui in May 2026, in which Yaashvi conducted the finale and represented India in a project with the Indian Embassy in Venezuela and El Sistema.

Jupiter Symphony, first movement, Mozart
Filarmonica Pitești
Romania
An orchestral conducting masterclass with the Filarmonica Pitești and Michalis Economou in October 2026, for which Yaashvi was selected. She was said to have created an explosion from the podium.
2025
Piano concerti programme
KM Music Conservatory
Goethe-Institut, Chennai
25 April 2025. Conducted in collaboration with KM Music Conservatory at the Goethe-Institut, a programme of Haydn, Mozart's Piano Concerto No. 23 in A major, the first movement of a Beethoven piano concerto, Debussy's two dances for harp and string orchestra and the second movement of Ravel's Piano Concerto in G major, together with Beethoven's Violin Concerto.
2025
High Scorers’ Concert
City Montessori School Chamber Orchestra
Lucknow
Conducted during her time as Conductor and Piano Instructor at City Montessori School, Lucknow (June 2025 to April 2026), where she built the school's orchestral and choral programmes. The concert brought together the school's chamber orchestra and choir in works including John Rutter, alongside the teachers' choir singing Prendi la vela.
2024
The Lost Woman
Middlesex Philharmonic and singers
St Mary’s Church, London
Sunday 10 March 2024, for International Women’s Day, the project's second performance. Fourteen works by fourteen women composers, including Florence Price, Chiquinha Gonzaga, Clémence de Grandval and Raphaella Aleotti, which Yaashvi directed as Artistic Director, conducted, played on piano, and arranged two of.
2023–24
